Community guidance mapping public detection content to ECC control intent. It is not an NCA‑endorsed compliance attestation, and evidence of a control is not proof of risk reduction. The Arabic ECC is the binding text; English here is a paraphrase aid.

Where coverage actually is

Detection content evidences technical defense controls. It cannot evidence policy, governance, or process controls — so honest coverage is concentrated in one domain and absent by design in the other four. We show that plainly rather than fake breadth.
